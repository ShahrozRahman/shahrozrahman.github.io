About Me

Bioinformatics Researcher & Computational Tool Developer

My bioinformatics experience spans functional genomics research, freelance biological data analysis, and open-source software development. I work with high-throughput sequencing, single-cell RNA-seq, genomic variants, and biomedical datasets, developing reproducible workflows in Python, R, and Linux. I have contributed to 10 publications, including first-author functional genomics studies, and built tools for omics analysis and machine-learning evaluation. I hold a BS in Bioinformatics from Government College University Faisalabad (CGPA 3.72/4.00), combining computational skills with hands-on laboratory experience to investigate biological systems and disease-associated molecular mechanisms.
